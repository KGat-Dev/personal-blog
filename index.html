About

I am a professional manager, a generalist by nature, and I love building all sorts of frameworks and solutions for unconventional problems. Over the past decade, following my curiosity has taken me through robotics, industrial automation, construction management and real estate development.

I read widely across psychology, decision-making, and operations, studying the seams where they intersect. Through my writing, I aim to share the systems behind consistent performance and how new technologies can optimize workflows, freeing up time for high-value strategic work.
